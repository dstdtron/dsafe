Secure Storage Safes

The Dsafe TM range is a stackable, secure, electronic storage system suitable for a variety of environments. Each compartment is controlled by an electric lock with integrated position status. A number of base rules govern the retrieval of stock such as tag or pin codes, delayed retrieval and storage interlocking. Stackable units can also be interlocked.

The system has been designed with robustness and a continuous operation in mind. Background electronic operations are performed by the PLC-ACC1 controller which has a proven track record for reliability and includes several safety features.

The storage unit has been designed with adequate safe overrides to facilitate continuous business operation in case of equipment malfunction. A quick overview of the storage compartment status can be viewed on the front LED panel. On-premise enrolment and detail diagnostic functions can be performed on a Mobile Phone with secure anti-theft software protection.
